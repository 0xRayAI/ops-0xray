How should I use these terms in my own team?

Use whichever names your team already says out loud, and keep a single glossary so each name means one thing. The concept is what matters: one accountable owner per action, a gate before risky steps, a trace per run, and a cap that stops new work. Our guides to map the agentic control plane and release gates for agents put several of these terms to work.

A note on two collisions: "AgentOps" is both a generic term and a product name, so we use it sparingly, and "Bifrost" also names an unrelated LLM gateway, so we say "our board" in public copy first.

What else do operators ask?

What is AI fleet ops?

It's the practice of running several AI agents as one accountable operation, with named owners, review gates, and a live work queue.

Is a control plane the same as an orchestration framework?

No. A framework runs agents; a control plane sets who owns the work, what is allowed, and where the record lives.

What does RACI mean for AI agents?

It assigns who does, who answers for, who advises on, and who hears about each action, with exactly one accountable human.
